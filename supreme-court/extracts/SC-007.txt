It is therefore clear that in cases of unauthorized use of
electricity amounting to theft, besides assessment
proceeding under Section 126 (1) of the 2003 Act, a
complaint can also be filed under Section 135 (1) of the
2003 Act and in such cases the Special Court is empowered
to determine the civil liability of the person concerned under
Section 154(5) of the 2003 Act. On such determination of
the civil liability by the Special Court, the excess amount, if
any, deposited by the person concerned, is to be refunded to
him. This implies that in cases of theft i.e. where a criminal
complaint is filed, the assessment made under Section 126
read with the provisions of the Supply Code is subject to the
final determination of the civil liability under Section 154(5)
of the 2003 Act.
