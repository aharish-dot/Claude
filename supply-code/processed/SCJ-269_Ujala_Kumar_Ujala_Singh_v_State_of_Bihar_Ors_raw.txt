ORAL ORDER
Heard the parties.
The petitioner has prayed for a limited direction to the respondent-
authorities to provide him with a new electricity connection. It is stated 
that only on grounds that the previous lease-holder of the petitioner 
had some outstanding against him that the connection is being denied. 
The action is purportedly taken under clause 4.15(vi) of the Bihar 
Electricity Supply Code, 2007 which provides as under:

“4.15. No Dues Certificate-
… …. … …
… …. … …
(vi) in case the electricity connection to the said premises was given 
with the consent of house owner, such person shall ensure the payment 
of all arrears/dues of electricity by the tenant before the tenant vacates 
the premises.
However the conditions mentioned under clause 4.15 above shall not 
apply if inconsistent with the provision of any higher court order or an 
order as a consequence to it.”
As is manifest from the clause 4.15(vi) itself that it is only in 
circumstances where the electricity connection has been provided to 
the lease-holder with the consent of the house-owner that he has been 
made liable for clearing the dues outstanding against the lease-holder. 
There is nothing on record to suggest that the electrical connection was 
provided with the consent of the house-owner, although it was orally 
submitted that the application for connection filed by the lease-holder 
bears the signature of the petitioner. A mere presence of signature on 
an application cannot signify consent unless it is expressed in such 
terms.
In the circumstances discussed, the denial of electrical connection to 
the petitioner is held unjustified. The concerned Executive Engineer or 
the authority concerned, is directed to provide connection to the 
petitioner 
within 
a 
period 
of 
four 
weeks 
from 
the 
date 
of 
receipt/production of a copy of this order.
The writ petition is allowed.
