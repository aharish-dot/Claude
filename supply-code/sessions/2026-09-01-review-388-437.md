3. **`citation_count` is prose + IK.** Allahabad PDFs have no IK doc-ids. Fingerprint now stores `ik_citation_count`, `text_citation_count`, and `citation_count = max(ik, text)`. Caption `X v. Y` and this case’s own Neutral Citation No. are excluded. The short-uncited gate (3 pages, 801–1500 words, uncited) now means **no authorities in the order**, not “no hyperlinks.” Pages ≤ 2 and words ≤ 800 stay short even with citations.

Listing-only stays off. Words ≤ 800 floor unchanged. Do not widen BILL into refusals.
