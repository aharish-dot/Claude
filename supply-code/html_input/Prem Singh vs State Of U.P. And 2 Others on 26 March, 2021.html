The dispute in the present petition is with regard to the assessment of the electricity bill.

It is open for the petitioner to approach the respondent no.3 namely the Executive Engineer (Rural), Dakshinanchal Vidyut Vitran Nigam Ltd, Fatehgarh, District- Farrukhabad by invoking provisions of Clause 6.5 of the U.P. Electricity Supply Code, 2005.

In view of the above, the wit petition is disposed of.
