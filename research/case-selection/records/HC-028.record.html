Facts & profile

A plastic-unit consumer with two connections was inspected, an FIR under Section 135 lodged, compounding cheques (Rs 15 lakh) encashed, and a Section 126 provisional and final assessment made under the U.P. Electricity Supply Code, 2005. A prior writ against recovery was dismissed as the consumer had a Section 127 appeal remedy; on filing that appeal, the Commissioner dismissed it for want of jurisdiction.

Issues framed

Interpretation of the Electricity Act

Provisions construed: s.126, s.127, s.135, s.154(6)

s.127ratiopost-2007 appellate-authority-jurisdiction §127 appeal
An order of assessment under Section 126 is appealable under Section 127 to the Commissioner of the Division (the notified appellate authority under the 14.7.2006 notification); the Commissioner was not justified in dismissing the appeal for want of jurisdiction.
“the Commissioner was not justified in dismissing the appeal of the petitioner on the ground that he has no jurisdiction to hear and decide the appeal of the petitioner under Section 127 of the Electricity Act.”— para 9
s.127ratiopost-2007 notified-appellate-forum §127 appeal
The State's 14.7.2006 notification confers on the Commissioner of the Division the power to hear and decide appeals under Section 127.
“State Government by its notification dated 14.7.2006 has conferred the powers to the Commission of the division to hear and decide the appeal under Section 127 of the Electricity Act.”— para 6
s.126 / s.135ratiopost-2007 s126-assessment-despite-fir §126 vs §135 §127 appeal
Even where an FIR is lodged under Section 135, the assessment is carried out under Section 126 (with the Section 127 appeal remedy), and the Special Court finally determines the civil liability under Section 154(6).
“A perusal of the aforesaid division bench judgements clearly shows that the petitioner has remedy of appeal under Section 127 of Electricity Act.”— para 9

Regulations / Supply Code construed

  • U.P. Government notification dated 14.7.2006 (Commissioner of Division as Section 127 appellate authority) — implements s.127 appeal (made under Electricity Act, 2003)
  • U.P. Electricity Supply Code, 2005 (final assessment) — implements s.126 procedure (made under Electricity Act, 2003)

Ratio & what was decided

§127 appeal HC-SB (Allahabad)applies
A Section 126 assessment is appealable under Section 127 to the notified appellate authority (in U.P., the Commissioner of the Division) even when an FIR under Section 135 exists; the appellate authority cannot decline jurisdiction, and the Special Court finally determines civil liability under Section 154(6).

Significance

An Allahabad authority correcting an appellate authority that wrongly refused jurisdiction over a Section 127 appeal: a Section 126 assessment is appealable to the Commissioner (per the 14.7.2006 notification) even where an FIR under Section 135 exists, with the Special Court finally determining civil liability under Section 154(6). Applies Sandeep Kesarwani (HC-003) and Sanjay Singh; references Seetaram on the Section 127 alternative-remedy rule.
