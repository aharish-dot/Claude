## OMB-001 — Representation No. C-19/2026
**Forum:** Electricity Ombudsman (Vidyut Lokpal), U.P. · **Disposed:** ~30.06.2026 · **Discom:** MVVNL / LESA, EUDD-Mahanagar, Lucknow · **OCR confidence:** medium (Hindi; re-OCR'd with hin pack)

- **Consumer / connection:** Smt. Shabina, wife of Mohammad Rais, of 525-A/046, Sector-A, Mahanagar, **Lucknow** — a **domestic** consumer; direct-metered.
- **Posture:** A non-compliance / execution move — the petitioner said the licensee had not carried out the **re-assessment** the Ombudsman had earlier ordered (Petition 113/2020, order dated 03.02.2021) in an alleged **theft** matter, and invoked s.142.
- **Held:** Not maintainable. Disputes concerning **theft / unauthorised use of electricity** and the assessment that follows fall outside the CGRF/Ombudsman's competence — they belong to the s.126/135 machinery and the appellate authority under s.127 / the Special Court. **Reg 3.10 of the 2022 CGRF & Ombudsman Regulations** expressly bars the forum from entertaining such complaints.
- **Ratio:** *The Ombudsman has no jurisdiction over theft/UUE assessment; the remedy lies under s.127 / the Special Court, and Reg 3.10 bars entertainment of the complaint.*
- **Tags:** `theft-jurisdiction-bar` · `s142-noncompliance` · `forum-competence`

## OMB-002 — Representation No. C-20/2026
**Forum:** Electricity Ombudsman, U.P. (Sanjay Srivastava) · **Disposed:** ~16.06.2026 · **Discom:** PVVNL, Saharanpur · **OCR confidence:** high (English)

- **Consumer / connection:** Maa Shakumbhari Plastic Udyog (proprietor Vipul Arora) — **industrial**, HV, billed on **kVAH** through a main + double-pole (check) meter.
- **Underlying subject:** A **metering / assessment** dispute — 2,00,638 kVAH assessed for 01.12.2019–08.06.2020 on a defective main meter; the earlier order (Rep. 21/2025, dated 05.12.2025) had already waived the surcharge for the licensee's negligence.
- **Posture:** A **review / recall** of the Ombudsman's *own* order.
- **Held:** Dismissed. The CGRF & Ombudsman Regulations contain **no power of review/recall**. Even importing the civil standard, a review under **CPC s.114 r/w Order XLVII Rule 1** lies only on *newly-discovered evidence not available with due diligence* or *an error apparent on the face of the record* — neither shown; the "new" facts were already dealt with (pp. 10–11 of the original order).
- **Ratio:** *The Electricity Ombudsman cannot review or recall its own order; a review lies only on CPC Order XLVII grounds, and re-argument of already-considered facts is not such a ground.*
- **Tags:** `no-review-power` · `cpc-order-47-standard` · `finality-of-orders`

## OMB-003 — Representation No. C-25/2026
**Forum:** Electricity Ombudsman, U.P. · **Disposed:** ~01.07.2026 · **Discom:** MVVNL, EDD-II Gauriganj (Tiloi), Amethi · **OCR confidence:** medium

- **Consumer / connection:** M/s Om Industries, a **rice mill** (proprietor Ram Babu) — **industrial, HV-2, 110 KVA**, Genus meter, multiplying factor 6.
- **Subject:** **Electricity-duty (E.D.) exemption** under the U.P. **Industrial Policy 2004**, and the licensee's failure to give effect to a **CGRF Ayodhya** judgment (Case 14/2021, dated 31.07.2023) directing re-assessment + duty benefit.
- **Posture:** Non-compliance / execution of the CGRF order (s.142).
- **Held (the one merits-side outcome):** The licensee is bound to implement the CGRF direction; the Ombudsman **directed compliance** — extension of the electricity-duty benefit and adjustment of **₹5,85,439.30** (against the petitioner's deposit of ₹5,988.20).
- **Ratio:** *A licensee must implement a CGRF direction granting electricity-duty exemption under the Industrial Policy; continued non-compliance is enforced and the benefit directed to be extended.*
- **Tags:** `electricity-duty-exemption` · `industrial-policy-2004` · `s142-enforcement` · `cgrf-order-compliance`

## OMB-004 — Representation No. 23/2026
**Forum:** Electricity Ombudsman, U.P. (Sanjay Srivastava) · **Disposed:** 07.07.2026 · **Discom:** PVVNL, EDD-III Loni, Ghaziabad · **OCR confidence:** high (English)

- **Consumer / connection:** Sri Alok Kumar @ Alok Sharma, resident of Flat A-508, **OXY Homes** society — **domestic**, but supplied through a **builder / deemed-franchisee single-point** arrangement with **prepaid** sub-metering.
- **Subject:** Alleged **over-recovery** by the society/builder through prepaid electricity billing — DG charges, maintenance, AMC, penalties — said to breach **Supply Code cl. 4.46(b)(i)** (deemed franchisee may recover only on a **"no profit, no loss"** basis). The CGRF (Loni) had dismissed on the sole ground that the appellant is **not a "consumer" u/s 2(15)** because the connection stands in his wife's name; the appeal (s.42(6)) relied on *Hari Chand v. HVPNL* (NCDRC) that a **beneficial user** is a consumer.
- **Held:** Disposed on the appellant's own **withdrawal application** — dismissed **with liberty** to refile before the CGRF in the **registered owner's** name, with a direction to decide all grievances. The substantive questions (consumer-definition; franchisee over-recovery) were **left undecided**.
- **Ratio:** *(none on merits)* — withdrawn with liberty; records but does not decide the s.2(15) beneficial-user question and the cl. 4.46(b)(i) no-profit-no-loss question.
- **Tags:** `consumer-definition-2(15)` · `single-point-supply` · `deemed-franchisee-4.46` · `no-profit-no-loss` · `withdrawn-liberty` · `builder-society-recovery`

## OMB-005 — Representation No. 15/2026
**Forum:** Electricity Ombudsman, U.P. (Sanjay Srivastava) · **Disposed:** ~09.07.2026 · **Discom:** MVVNL, EDD-Tanda, Ambedkar Nagar · **OCR confidence:** high (English)

- **Consumer / connection:** M/s J.K. Industries (proprietor Mrs. Seema Jaiswal) — **industrial, MV-6, 80 HP**, on a **rural** 11 kV feeder; an ISO/ISI-certified hinge manufacturer employing ~120 workers.
- **Subject:** **Supply reliability** — daily supply allegedly cut from ~6 hours to ~2 hours from 20.11.2020, forcing closure; claim for **₹7.5 crore compensation** + restoration of 9 AM–5 PM supply. (Licensee: supply depends on feeder/roster; the connection was permanently disconnected on 24.04.2023 for **dues of ₹2,14,119**, so the petition is "surrogate.")
- **Posture:** Direct complaint to the Ombudsman **after withdrawing an Allahabad HC writ** (Writ-C 12243/2025) with liberty to approach the Ombudsman.
- **Held:** Not maintainable **at this stage**. The Ombudsman exercises **only appellate/supervisory** jurisdiction over the CGRF and has **no original jurisdiction**; a compensation claim for deficient standards of performance must first go through the **compensation mechanism in cl. 8 of the UPERC (Standards of Performance) Regulations, 2019** (cl. 8.4.4 lets the consumer come to the Ombudsman only *after* the CGRF stage). A High Court's grant of "liberty to approach the Ombudsman" does not confer jurisdiction the forum otherwise lacks. Dismissed with liberty to refile after availing the proper remedy.
- **Ratio:** *The Ombudsman has no original jurisdiction; SoP-compensation claims must be routed through the CGRF under cl. 8 of the 2019 Regulations first, and HC "liberty" cannot create jurisdiction.*
- **Tags:** `no-original-jurisdiction` · `appellate-only` · `exhaust-cgrf-first` · `sop-compensation-clause8` · `hc-liberty-no-jurisdiction` · `supply-hours`

### Cross-order observations
- **4 / 5 disposed on a threshold ground** (jurisdiction / review-power / withdrawal); only **OMB-003** reached relief, and that was **enforcement** of a CGRF order, not fresh merits.
- **Two jurisdiction doctrines recur:** subject-matter bar (theft/UUE → s.126/135/127 — OMB-001) and hierarchy/exhaustion (appellate-only; exhaust CGRF/SoP — OMB-005), plus no-power-of-review (OMB-002) and the consumer-definition gate (OMB-004).
- **Recurring anchors:** EA s.2(15), 42(5)/(6), 126/135/127, 142, 181; Supply Code 2005 cl. 4.46; CGRF & Ombudsman Regs 2007/2022 (Reg 3.10); SoP Regs 2019 cl. 8/8.4.4; CPC Order XLVII.
- **"Non-compliance of a prior order" (s.142)** is a distinct recurring sub-genre (OMB-001, OMB-003).
